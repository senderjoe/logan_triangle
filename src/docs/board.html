Triangle carrier board: component side

ElectroCookie half-size protoboard (30 rows, A–J) · v4, 1 Oct 2026 · needs CARRIER_BOARD true in config.h

Placement (board coordinates as printed)

Micro2 × 17-way female header sockets in C1–C17 and G1–G17, USB end at row 1. Fit the top centre mounting screw first: it sits under the Micro.
ENC-RJST-XH 5-pin in J3–J7, pin 1 at J3. Right motor encoder.
ENC-LJST-XH 5-pin in J11–J15, pin 1 at J11. Left motor encoder.
DRV-RJST-XH 4-pin in A4–A7, pin 1 at A4. Right driver.
DRV-LJST-XH 4-pin in A14–A17, pin 1 at A14. Left driver.
BTNJST-XH 2-pin in A9–A10, pin 1 at A9. Start button (optional).
D11N5819: anode in A20, cathode (band) in the left + rail by row 20.
C1100 µF 16 V across the left rails by row 22: + leg in the + rail.
Q12N2222 (or 2N3904): E in J21, B in J22, C in J23, flat face towards column I. Holds the drivers off until the program starts.
R1 R2R1 4.7 kΩ from I18 to the right + rail by row 18. R2 1 kΩ from H18 to H22.
Buck5 V buck module on foam tape in the empty area (rows 24–30). OUT+ lead to B20, OUT− lead to the left − rail by row 21. IN+ / IN− from the 12 V supply (own pair).

  • The encoder pin order has changed from the prototype: crimp to this table. Cut back ORG, GRN and WHT and insulate each.
  • Driver cables follow the DM320T's P1 order. Motor leads go straight to the drivers.

How it works

  • Each connector sits on the same strips as the Micro pins it uses, so no signal needs a wire: the copper joins them.
  • Where a connector needs 5 V or GND instead, that strip is cut between the connector and the Micro, and a short link on the underside joins the connector pin to a rail (page 2).
  • The Micro pins on cut strips (A1, VIN, the left GND, D9, D8, RST) are left unused. A0 is cut from DRV-R ENA and drives Q1 instead.

Build and test

  • Before anything: check with a meter that no + rail is joined to a − rail.
  • Cuts first, with a sharp knife or a 3 mm drill bit twisted by hand. Check with a meter that each one is open.
  • Then: mounting screw under the Micro, sockets (plug the Micro in to hold them straight while soldering), XH headers, D1, C1, R1, R2, Q1, buck leads, links 1–14. Links 11–13 are insulated wire along the bare channel between columns E and F.
  • Before fitting the Micro: check the + rails to the − rails isn't a short. Connect 12 V to the buck: both + rails should read about 4.6–5.1 V (set an adjustable buck to 5.3 V). Check each connector's 5V and GND pins.
  • Q1 check, Micro out, 12 V on: both DRV ENA pins read under 0.3 V to GND (drivers held off).
  • With the Micro fitted: set CARRIER_BOARD true in config.h, run the test sketch and use t to check each PUL and DIR at the driver end.
  • D1 stops the buck and a USB lead fighting, so USB can stay plugged in with the 12 V on.
